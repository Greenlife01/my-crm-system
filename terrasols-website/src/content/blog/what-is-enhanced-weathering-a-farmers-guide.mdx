If you've heard the term "Enhanced Rock Weathering" and wondered what it actually means for your field, you're not alone. Here's the simple version.

## What happens on your land

Terrasols delivers finely crushed basalt rock powder to your farm, free of cost. We spread it across your fields using the same equipment your crops are used to — there's no change to how you plant, irrigate, or harvest.

Over the following months and years, rainwater and naturally occurring CO₂ in the air react with the basalt. This is the same process that shapes mountains over geological time, except we speed it up by crushing the rock into a fine powder, dramatically increasing its surface area.

## What you get

- **Zero cost.** Basalt, transport, spreading labour, and lab testing are all covered by Terrasols.
- **Soil health.** Basalt supplies calcium, magnesium, and potassium, and helps buffer soil pH.
- **Carbon income.** A share of the revenue from verified carbon credit sales flows back to you.
- **No disruption.** Your farming practices stay exactly the same.

## What we measure

Our field team collects soil, water, and crop samples regularly, working with laboratories at IARI-PUSA and JNU to verify how much carbon has been removed. This data is submitted under the Isometric EW Protocol v1.2 — one of the strictest carbon removal standards in the world.
