Carbon removal buyers face a hard problem: how do you know a claimed tonne of CO₂ removal actually happened? For Enhanced Rock Weathering, the answer depends entirely on the rigor of the measurement, reporting, and verification (MRV) protocol behind it.

## Why we chose Isometric EW v1.2

Isometric's Enhanced Weathering Protocol v1.2 is widely regarded as the most conservative and scientifically rigorous ERW methodology available today. It requires:

- **Primary data collection** across soil, water, and crop samples — not modelled estimates alone
- **Conservative uncertainty deductions** that err against overclaiming
- **Third-party laboratory verification**, in our case through partners at IARI-PUSA and JNU
- **Full traceability** from rock source to field application to sampling

## What this means for buyers

When you purchase a Terrasols carbon removal credit, it has been verified against a protocol designed to withstand scrutiny — not just from auditors, but from the scientific community. This matters more every year, as buyers face growing pressure to prove additionality and permanence in their climate claims.

## Where we're headed

We're building DHARA, our primary-data dMRV platform, to make this process faster and more transparent as we scale from 500 tonnes toward 50,000 tonnes of annual supply. Rigor doesn't have to mean slow — it has to mean trustworthy.
